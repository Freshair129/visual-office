# Functional department floor redesign

The owner's 30 October correction rejects the repeated room shell with renamed
zones. Each department needs a distinct arrangement that supports its own work.
The owner also permits a larger floor plate where circulation or room use is
cramped. This note records the requested revision and implementation sequence;
it does not record acceptance of the finished result.

The preceding contact baseline remains useful: the frozen 16 × 10 m plans ran
96 hardware-rendered cases, completing 86, with every default desk case passing.
The ten refusals were incompatible scaled chair fixtures. All 547 local tests
passed on that earlier composition. Those results do not validate the revised
floor geometry below.

## Design

Use a shared 24 × 16 m building plate, with an aligned enclosed lift/service core
at the rear and enough circulation for the selected work programme. Keep the
lift datum consistent between floors; interior partitions, room counts,
adjacencies, doors and workstation groupings must vary by use. Geometry
signatures excluding labels and the common core must distinguish all fifteen
floors. Warehouse remains an office planning simulation.

Examples of the intended differences are public intake and private case rooms
for Customer, production and campaign review settings for Marketing, separate
source preparation and quiet citation review for Knowledge, and count/variance
settings for Inventory. Staff workstations, visitor routes, collaborative rooms
and quiet/private rooms should have plausible connections and furniture.
Keep a 1.8–2 m main circulation route, unobstructed doors and usable side access
to each paired desk and chair. Preserve the verified 1.20 m workstation spacing.

Keep floor, department, role and existing workstation semantic identities.
New default transforms may change under this redesign. Existing saved rooms
remain authored snapshots; applying a new plan is an undoable draft followed
by explicit Save. New rooms and structure receive distinct design identities.
The functional guide links proposed room use to existing role/workflow designs;
it does not instantiate agents or execute a business workflow.
